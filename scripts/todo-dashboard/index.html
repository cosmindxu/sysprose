Open items by severity

After each merge that changed the list. Wrong answer: a false verdict, a lost edit or a page that differs from its saved file. Weaker: sound, but gives up a reading it could make. Other: wording, speed, tests, UI polish, items waiting on a decision.

Found and closed, cumulative since the review

Counting starts at the merge that brought in the 14 October review, which itself filed most of its items. The gap between the lines is the growth of the list. Narrowing an item to its residual counts as neither.

Found and closed at each merge

One pair of bars per merge. Above the line: items the merge's reviews and gates filed. Below: items it closed. The darker part is the wrong-answer class.

By wave

A wave is a set of branches run and merged together. Found per closed above 1 means the list grew. The last row is incomplete: several of its branches have not merged yet.

Where the wrong-answer and weaker-reading findings come from

Items filed since the review that were not a narrowing of an earlier item, by what the entry says about its origin.

How the counts were made

Each point is the state of the list in the repository's history after a merge that changed it. An item is a checkbox entry. One that disappears counts as closed and one that appears as found. A pair that is the same problem narrowed to its residual counts as neither; two auditors checked those pairs against the diffs, and two stale re-adds made by branch merges on 12 October are removed. Severity and origin were assigned from each entry's text by two independent passes, and a third pass settled their disagreements.

Open items by how they close

Each open item sits in one class, set by the coordinator from the item's text and the work in flight. A class says what the item still needs before it can close, not how severe it is. Rollups group many small weaker readings, and close only when every reading in them is built or ruled.

Open items by SysML v2 view

Rows are the SysML v2 language areas, then the tool's own areas. The grade says how much of the view the tool decides formally today (assessment of 5 November on main 5ddc1e2, checked by a skeptic): Decided, Partly, Traced only (listed or linked, never judged), Not covered. The bar counts the open items whose main view is that row, coloured by how they close. A view with a weak grade and no bar is a gap that no open item addresses yet.
