How this was computed. Rows come from 02-omg-standard-reference (§2.2 metaclasses, §3.2 textual areas, §4.1 view kinds, §5.7 API groups) and the class register (docs/CONSTRAINT-CLASSES.md). A TXT row is read when the corpus uses the construct (parse conformance proven by corpus.test.ts); a CON row is decided when its class level is differential-tested or tested, partial when experimental, refused when fenced/unpinned. Weights are corpus usage (TXT) or counted rows (CON). GRAPH/API/KERML rows are unmeasured here; the app's 17 rendered views and the API/OSLC PSMs have their own tests (not folded into the percentages). Phase 2 replaces the unmeasured rows with pinned probes across the four surfaces.
